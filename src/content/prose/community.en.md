October 2024, Hakodate.

When I was a student, someone I knew told me about YAPC::Hakodate. I wanted to go, but I did not know a single person there. Signing up as day-of staff seemed like a way not to stand out — that was my first reason for getting involved.

Once I was actually doing the work, I found that running the event was interesting in its own right. Since then I have turned up at conferences that are looking for staff.

If I had to give one reason for organising conferences, it is that they let me meet people.

<p class="letter__separator" aria-hidden="true">* * *</p>

Hono Conference began for me when I saw the person who runs Kichijoji.pm putting it together and offered to help. I was on the core staff in 2025, and in 2026 I took over as chair from my predecessor.

What I do as chair is make decisions, talk to sponsors and manage the budget. Beyond that, it is keeping the core staff in a state where each of them can move.

How deeply I am involved differs from event to event. Sometimes I am only day-of staff; sometimes I am there from the planning stage. My rule is to go where there are not enough hands.
