title: Part Tech Lead
summary: In-house development of a business application, and its maintenance
---

I joined an internal DX project that had been stalled for two years. By the time I arrived the original plan had gone stale, so I started by rebuilding the plan itself.

- Re-established contact with the departments that would use the system, and redefined the requirements against the current situation
- Introduced Slack and a ticket system so that feedback from those departments could be picked up immediately
- Designed and built the business application in parallel, and shipped it in under a year

Since release I have also handled maintenance and operations. The new system ran alongside the existing one while we migrated in stages.

- Moved access control to RBAC. Old and new ran side by side, and a feature flag let us swap them without downtime
- Managed the infrastructure with Terraform
- Set up fault detection and alerting
- Turned recurring operational work — log review, dependency updates, vulnerability response — into an AI agent skill
